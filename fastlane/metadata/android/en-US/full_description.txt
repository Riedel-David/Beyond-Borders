Beyond Borders is a travel tracker and visited countries map with a built-in passport visa checker. It works offline: no account, no ads, no tracking, no analytics. Its only network use is an optional weekly download of updated visa data from GitHub Pages over HTTPS, on unmetered networks (plus a manual Check now); you can switch it off in Settings, and no account or identifier is sent.

It exists because of one annoyance. Most travel trackers select more than you ask for: tap Denmark and you get Greenland, tap France and you get French Guiana. In Beyond Borders those are separate places, and the app has no parent-child link between places at all, so it cannot cascade a selection by accident.

TRAVEL MAP
• Country tracker for 252 places on a world map, tap to select
• Mark each as Been, Lived or Want, so it doubles as a bucket list
• Add as many visits as you like to a place, each with a year and an optional month, shown as a timeline on the place card
• Quick add: tick off the 15 most visited countries in one go
• Region-completion heatmap shows where you have the most left to see
• Works in airplane mode: the map is bundled, with no tile server or API key

VISA LENS
• See where your passport goes, from almost 200 passports (or skip choosing one and just track your travels)
• Colour the map by entry requirement: visa-free, travel authorisation, visa on arrival, e-visa, visa required, check requirements
• Countries your own government advises against travelling to are labelled as such instead of showing a visa answer
• Hold two passports and the easier of the two answers wins
• Add a travel partner's passport and see where you can both go: the stricter answer wins, and the row says who sets the limit
• Place cards link to the UK government's foreign travel advice and, where available, the German Federal Foreign Office warning; both open in your browser
• Visa data is a bundled snapshot, optionally refreshed weekly, not legal advice, and it can be out of date. Always confirm entry rules with the official government source before you travel.

NOT A GOVERNMENT APP
Beyond Borders is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or representing any government or government agency, and it does not issue or process visas.

SOURCES OF VISA AND TRAVEL INFORMATION
• Visa requirements: Visa-requirements dataset (derived from Wikipedia, CC BY-SA 4.0), https://github.com/Riedel-David/Visa-requirements
• Travel advice links: UK government foreign travel advice (GOV.UK), opened in your browser
• Travel warnings: German Federal Foreign Office (Auswärtiges Amt), https://www.auswaertiges-amt.de/de/reiseundsicherheit/reise-und-sicherheitshinweise
• For binding entry rules, use the official source of the country you are visiting: its foreign ministry, immigration authority or embassy.

PASSPORT LIST AND STATS
• Searchable list with filters, a wishlist toggle and an A-Z rail
• Per-continent progress, your least-covered continent and the next milestone
• Statistics screen with a collapsible visits-by-year chart
• Badges, including landlocked countries and island nations, three levels each

SHARE
• Export your map as an image, captioned with your numbers, in one of three styles
• Sharing only happens when you choose to, through the Android share sheet

HOME-SCREEN WIDGET
• Pick the headline metric and card background for each widget

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
• Everything is stored on your device
• Export and import your history, visit dates included, as JSON
• One-tap "Delete everything" reset
• If Android's Auto Backup is on, a snapshot of your data is included in your device backup and offered back after a reinstall. Switch it off in Android settings if you prefer.

LANGUAGES AND LOOK
• English, Deutsch, Español, Français, Português, Русский, Українська
• Light, dark and pure black themes
